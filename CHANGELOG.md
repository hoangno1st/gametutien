# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-07

First production-ready web release of Tiên Lộ Idle.

- Auto combat with HP/MP, active skills, enemy archetypes, elites, rare events and multi-phase bosses.
- Nine chapters with 50 stages each, distinct identities, backgrounds and progression requirements.
- Equipment crafting, rarity rolls, random stats, stat unlock/lock/reroll, enhancement and salvage.
- Alchemy, pills, buffs and catalyst-assisted crafting.
- Cultivation realms, breakthroughs, Artifacts, stars/passives, Techniques and milestones.
- Quests, achievements, daily tasks and guided tutorial.
- Versioned local-first save, migration, import/export and offline cultivation.
- Responsive PixiJS UI, notifications, accessibility settings, PWA shell and fatal-error recovery.
- Asset, analytics, account and cloud-save abstraction foundations for later releases.
